Quick guide

How to play

Each player secretly distributes exactly 100 Power Points across nine territories. The goal is to win more territories than your opponent.

1. Place your bids

Enter a whole number from 0 to 100 in each box. You may put all 100 points on one territory, split them across a few, or spread them across all nine. Unused boxes can stay at 0.

2. Take turns privately

  1. Player 1 enters bids and locks the board.
  2. Pass the device to Player 2. Player 1's bids are hidden.
  3. Player 2 enters bids and locks the board.

3. Compare each territory

  • Higher bid captures that territory.
  • Equal bids mean neither player captures it.
  • Most territories wins the match.
  • If territory counts are equal, whoever captures the center, E, wins. If neither captures E, the match is a draw.
